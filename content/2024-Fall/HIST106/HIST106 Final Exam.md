## Question One

**How did average people try to change American society in the 1950s and beyond? What did they accomplish (or also fail to accomplish)?** You can refer to specific social changes, laws, Supreme Court decisions, and more. You can discuss the Civil Rights Movement, but also the other rights movements we studied.

Although much of progressive activists' hard work came to fruition in the Warren court's groundbreaking decisions of the 50s and 60s, these changes were dependent on the efforts of average Americans. Social currents like the civil rights and Chicano movements would not have been able to rise to the prominence they reached were it not for the actions of the everyday citizen.
