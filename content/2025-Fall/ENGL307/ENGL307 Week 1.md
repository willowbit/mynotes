## 8.26

**How to succeed:**
- Read assignment directions & look at examples
- Use AP style Top 10 list. Print it out.
- Read!
- Never write in first or second person, use a rhetorical question, or a semicolon

**What is journalism?**
- Holding people accountable, particularly government officials
- Reporting on the events of the day in an objective way
- Trying to inform the public and how it impacts them
- Writing about life in a way that connects people

**Core values of journalism:**
- Accuracy
- Independence
- Objectivity
- Humanity
- Accountability
