## 5.5

Magic as "Critical Category of Distinction"
- utlity in media: versatile tool to categorize and distinguish things/beings, practices, & ways of thinking = "powerful represenational device"
- commentary intersections (self vs. other): gender, disability, class/status, citizen/alien, human/non-human

Fairytales as Source in Popular Imagination: pop culture witch origins...
- fairytale witches: evil, turn people into animals, cannibals, attack children
- problematic entertainment: traditionally used to incite the marginalization and discrimination against real people (women, elderly, disabled, ethnic minorities)

Pop Culture Witchcraft as Tool of Commentary
- creates a hidden subgroup within society to safely explore contemporary social issues
- so what: wester society's "internal others" → use to define our values

Focus #1: explore relationships and the role/life of women (especially exploring domestic life as domestication)
- symbol and example: broom and Bewitched (tv)
- Samantha the witch wife and Darrin the non-witch husband → husband does not want wife to practice craft (reinforce nuclear family roles), but she often saves / helps him (critique of patriarhcal culture as humor)

Love Spells

- timing and tools: Fridays best on a new moon; rose water, place targets hair under dripping water to wash away resistance
