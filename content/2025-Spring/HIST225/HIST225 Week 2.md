## 2.10

Control, or responding to lack of control (environment)

**Ancient Near East (ANE)**

Mespopotamia (focus: c. 4000-330 BCE)

ANE → Mesopotamia = mixed region of empires over time (modern day Iraq)
- Fertile Crescent Region: Tigris-Euphrates rivers = "cradle of civilization"
- Permenant settlement = cultural innovation: transition from hunter-gathering to agricultural societies (Neolithic Revolution: 10000 BCE onwards)

**Magic in the Ancient Near East**

Cosmotheology: worshipping cosmos as a whole (the Oneness)
- multiplicity of gods based in nature/concepts
- humans (clay + blood of gods) = born to serve gods
- daily life is a battle: good vs. evil → most magic = defensive

Magician/Exorcist (Asipu): hereditary & skilled occupation (male)
- temple rituals (cleaning/feed statues, installing priests)
- rituals for kings (installing them, protecting their reign)
- fighting evil (demons/witches/curses) & healing (medicine)

Developed divination as science: *divine messages from movement of heavens*
- astrology (star lore) & extispicy (reading animal entrails)
- zodiac (460 BCE) → 410 BCE 1st horoscope to predict individual's fate
