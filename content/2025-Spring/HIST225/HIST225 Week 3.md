## 2.17

**Spell:** words or formula that have magic power & impact when recited or performed

**Enchant:** to attract, delight, charm; to cast a spell on
- Old French → Latin: *incanto* (sing)

**Curse:** a supernatural detriment or hinderance; prayer that harm may befall someone; cause of great harm

**Binding Magic via Inscribed Objects:** formulas to invoke supernatural powers to do action or control others
- defixio/kortadesmos → to bind/fasten down
- thin sheets of lead/lead alloys or figurines w/ nails @ liminal places like tombs/doorways

**The 4-Fold Relationship of a Curse**
- client, specialist, god/ghost victim
- chthonic deities & unburied dead

**Thematic Trends:** Cursing (Greek & Roman) vs. Protection (Jewish)
- Competition
- Matters of the heart
- Protection/revenge

### Chinese Magic

**"This-World" / Present Life Focus, Not Gods/Transcendent World:**
- focus: family, not gods → human ancestors ensure well-being of living (*deserve reverence*)
- worship: tombs, temples, altars, mountain tops (*di:* divine creative force)
- magical presumption: people participate directly in universe good or evil) → help it come into being
- aspects: cycle of five elements (wood-fire-metal-water-earth); balance of yin-yang & flows of energy (*qi*)

**Two Main Ways of Conversing with the Dead:**
- divination via oracle bones (scapulimancy): burn/light → read cracks
- pay respects via feasts: maintain good will so you can ask questions/get help → offer favorite foods in bronze cooking vessel (*ding*) & basin (*gui*)

**Chinese Astrology** (c. 1,000 BCE): systemic observation of heavens to see patterns → 5 element theory, colours, animals
- course of life & character determined by hour, day, year of birth
- zodiac/horoscope: 12-year cycle (represented by animals)
