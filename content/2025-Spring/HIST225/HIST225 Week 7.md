## 3.17

Post-Second Temple (70 CE) to the Babylonian Talmud (500 CE):
- core: God's revelation (Torah) = two forms → written & oral → rabbis ("teachers") = produced & memorized oral form
- text(s) Talmud = interpretations of Written Torah, containing Mishnah (systemized oral law), Midrash (oral commentary on HB)
- so what: bible must be interpreted to apply to real life & changing times (e.g. what does "no work" on shabbat mean?)

Perspective Shift: Value of Magic/Miracle Depends Upon Actor & Purpose
- Good vs. bad depends on who is acting & its purpose, not the act itself
- merit-derived power: holy men earn via Torah knowledge & pious life
- "living Tora Scrolls": rabbis embody the thought & word of God

Honi-the-Circle-Drawer (1st c. BCE): On one occasion when God did not send rain well into the winter, he [Honi] drew a circle in the dust, stood inside it, and informed God that he would not move until it rained. When it began to drizzle, Honi told God that he was not satisfied and expected more rain; it then began to pour. He explained that he wanted a clam rain, at which point the rain calmed to a normal rain.

Christianity

"A Christian cannot employ magic" (Jerome): avoid connections
- reverse critique: turn accusations of magic back on Greco-Roman practitions (mutual rivalry)
- accusations of magic used to marginalize opponents, even other Christians (ritual censure & competitions)

Hagiography: stories of saints' lives
- asceticism (self denial) = virtuous act (humility) → live in desert at edges of community (make own food, pray continuously)
- purpose: inspire & instruct readers → conversion & imitation (cf. Jesus's own trial in the wilderness)
- so what: saints need only willpower to fight temptations and demons

Late Antique Developments in Demonology
- daimones: Greco-Roman animating forces/spirits (local/lower gods) → Christians now call demons in disguise
- St. Augustine (4th c. CE): "demons" trick with false knowledge because only God has foreknowledge (gives to prophets)
- purpose: God allows demons to do this → test human faith

## 3.19

7th c. CE: the birth of a geographically-oriented religious movement
- Muhammad (570-632), God's final prohpet (pbuh: "peace be upon him") → Muslim community (ummah) founded 622
- islam (Arabic): salaam "peace" (= "submission")
- focus: submission to God via prayer → go to mosque (masjid): "place of prostration" & respond to call to prayer (adhan): purify self, turn to Mecca, pray w/ entire body
- basic message of prayer (5x a day): "God is great, there is no other god (witness), Muhammad is God's messenger"
- text(s): Qur'an (God's Revelation to Muhammad via Gabriel), Hadith (Muhammad's teachings), & HB (recognize Jesus as a prophet)

Magic (sihr): illicit & harmful!
- taught to mankind by evil spirits (shayatin → djinn)
- suras of refuge (ch. 113-4 of the Qur'an): "Say I seek refuge in the Lord" (cf. Psalm 91) = prayers of protection, esp. against witches

Djinn: pre-Islamic supernatuarl creatures in Arabic mythology
- Qur'an: another created species like humans that either recognize God (faithful) or do not (unfaithful)
- Invisible/can change form (fire based) → tend to stay with their own kind (desert) but can play tricks
