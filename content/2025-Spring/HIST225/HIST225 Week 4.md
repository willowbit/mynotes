## 2.24

Pythia, High Priestess of the Temple of Apollo
- speaks for Apollo via divine "possession" (*enthusiasmos*)
- AKA" The Delphic Oracle

The Life of the Oracle
- sits on tripod delivers Apollo's prophesies (answers questions) while inhaling vapors
- "cries" interpreted & put into verse (poetry) → often ambiguous in meaning
- performs only once a month
- evidence: 615 prophesies

Classical Period (490-323 BCE): height of Greek power and culture
- organization around the *polis*: fiercely independent city-states that make up *Hellas* (e.g., Athens, Sparta) VS. foreign invaders (Persians)

Social Value of Ancient Greek Magic
- competitive city states & society: achieve *arete* (excellence)
- cultural anxiety: respond to lack of control in life
- shame-honor binary: envy over others' success and satisfaction

Examples by Type/Service:
- communication
- products
- transformations

*Pharmakeia:* using herbs to make medicines, potions, poisons
- *pharmakon:* a drug → the power to harm AND heal (depending on dosage)

## 2.26

**Divination:** the art of discovering secrets/future by means beyond the normal or natural
- *dīvīnō* "I foresee"
- gods respond to questions via oracles (*ōrāculum:* "divine announcements") → Delphic Oracle

**Augury:** interpretation of omens/signs, especially the behaviours of birds
- Romans: figure out will of gods before major actions

**Haruspicy:** divination via sacrificial animal entrails (especially the liver)
- Etruscan origins (pre-Romans in Italy)

### Ancient Rome (c. 625 BCE - 476 CE)
- militaristic culture & imperial expansion: Pax Romana (*Roman Peace*) Pax Deorum (*Peace of the Gods*) → a period of "peace & prosperity" (27 BCE - 180 CE)

**Traditional Ancestral Religion in the Classical World → the Public Cult**
- tied to political life
- rituals & festivals → Panhellenic vs. local vs. family
- ex. Olympian pantheon, local gods, household shrines
- contractual relationship with gods: give gods what they deserve → leave humanity alone OR help in present life, not about belief or salvation after death
- Romans more animistic (*spirits in everything*)

**Roman Magic: based off Greek magic**
- insider vs. outsider logic: help yourself (crops, health) VS. "foreign" use
- Skepticism of false/improper religion & medical practices
- practice seen as opposite to public cult

#### Greco-Roman Miracles

**Greco-Roman Wonderment, not Faith**
- *thauma:* "a wonder, marvel, something strange" & *signum:* "a sign, mark, or symbol" → observe something out of the ordinary
- polytheism not about conversion

**Purpose of Miracles:** *Two options*
- signpost a crisis because of divine offence
- expansion of local pantheon → importing new god = establish new sanctuary

### Wheel of the Year (Wicca)

**The 8 Shabbats: earth is a wheel on an axis**
1. Samhain (10/13): The New Year
    - The final harvest/prep for winter
    - liminal: dead & fae
2. Yule/Solstice (12/21-12/22): The Rebirth (of the god)
    - Carnival (reversal)
    - leisure & feasting (meat)
3. Imbolic (1/31-2/2): The First Light
    - Fire+ water (purification), quickening
    - Goddess **Brighid** (exhalted)
5. Beltane (4/30 or 5/1): True Consumation
    - Sexual union: god (sun) + goddess (spring)
    - Bonfire + "a-maging"
6. Litha (6/20-6/21): True Culmination
    - Midsummer
    - Green may → cut down (sacrifice), Stonehenge
7. Lughnased (8/1): The Decline
    - First harvest & handfasting (marriage)
    - Feasting (corn, wheat); county fairs (live stock)
8. Maboon (9/22-9/23): The Harvest
    - Second harvest & underworld
    - Descent & resurrection of child (male/female)

### Cast Study: Yuletide Goat (Julbocu)

Traditional scandanavian Festival marks Sun's path: with Capricorn (sun's rebirth @ Solstice)
- Traditional winter sacrifice: kid goat in honor of Saturn
- Norse Myth: Thor's chariot goats → killed and revived
- Becomes a symbolic ritual: young man dresses up as goat with gifts
- Positive appropriation: straw sacrifice (replacement effigy)
- Bundle of last harvest's wheat: contains magic of last harvest spirit
