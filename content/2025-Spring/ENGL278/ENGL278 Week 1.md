## 2.3

Ferdinand de Saussure's "Signs"
- Two interelated parts
	- Signifier
	- Signified

C.S. Peirce's Types of Signs
- Symbol
	- A signifier with an arbitrary relationship to the signified
	- Conventional, agreed upon
	- Ex: red Octogon, also words
	- General, not specific
- Index
	- Actual connection
	- Ex: cat's paw print
	- More specific
- Icon
	- Visual similarity, not arbitrary
	- Ex: drawing of a cat

**Connotation vs. Denotation**

Connotation = figurative language
- Metaphor (or simile): comparison
	- metaphors are imperfect
- Metonymy: associate detail or substitute naming
- Synecdoche: part-for-whole or whole-for-part
	- Car as "wheels"
	- "All hands on deck"
