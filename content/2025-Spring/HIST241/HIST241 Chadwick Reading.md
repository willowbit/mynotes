## Ch 1: From Jerusalem to Rome

### The Jewish Background

- Circumcision: symbolism of covenant made with Abraham
- Jews believed:
	- God's election of Israel as an exclusive society
	- Israel called to exercize priestly function to mankind as a whole
- Negative attitude towards pagan religion
	- cult of evil spirits
- Foreign domination & poor Palestinian economy → general emigration of Jews all over the Mediteranean world
	- Dispersion, Diaspora in greek/roman society
	- Always easier to let Jews have their own way
- Gentile: non-Jewish, Christian. Heathen, pagan.
- Gentiles attracted by monotheism
	- Purity of Jewish morality
	- Antiquity & style of sacred books
	- Judaism stood for chastity and stable family life
	- Works of charity
- ASK: Babylonian Deportation
- No more prophets → depend on book!
	- The Septuagint
		- Version of seventy translators
		- Sponsored by king Ptolemy Philadephus of Egypt

### The Earliest Church

- St Matthew's Gospel poses Jesus as *the new Moses*!!!
	- Come not to destroy, but to fulfil (Matthew 5-6)
- Christianity appeared to be a simple Judaic sect
- Pharisees wanted to **preserve religious and theocratic character** of Jewish life against Hellenistic influences
- Sadduccees held only to Mosaic law, not bound by scribal tradition
	- **Rejected belief of resurrection from the dead** (incompatible with Christ)
	- Disagreed with Pharisees about afterlife

#### Essenes
- Rigidly separatist society
- Dead Sea Scrolls
- Resembled early church
- Some rejected arms, some Zealots
- Location became scene of Jewish war (66-70)
- Rejected slavery, incompativle with equality of all men
- Precise about keeping sabbath day

#### Spread of Christianity
- Appealed to diverse groups
	- Pharisee's sense of seriousness
		- Became associated with the movement
	- Ordinary Jew's feeling of being overwhelmed by nit-picky tradition
- However
	- Did not appeal to Sadducees (did not believe in resurrection)
	- Did not encourage nationalistic Zealots
	- Too revolutionary for Jewish "Establishment" **(?)**
- Paul of Tarsus (Saul)
	- Meets Jesus on road to Damascus
	- Dominant figure in non-Jewish mission
	- *The apostle of the Gentiles*
	- Better at writing letters than talking!
	- Beef with Peter?!?!?
- Peter
	- Nominated as rock on which the church was to be built (Matt. xvi, 18)

### The Gentile Church

Everybody knew three things about Jews:
1. They would not be associated with any pagan cult
2. They refused to eat any meat that had been offered in sacrifice to pagan gods and all pork
3. They circumcised their male infants

How were these traditions to carry over to new Christian converts?
- Conservative group said yes, must comply to all, including circumcision
- Universalists claimed that these were limited to the Jewish people.
	- "In Christ God had acted for the reconciliation of humanity, **to break down the barriers** (...) between one man and another"
- **Pentateuch:** the first five books of Jewish and Christian Scriptures.
- Council of Jerusalem (Acts xv)
	- Compromise favouring the universalists (Paul and the Gentile Christians)
	- Gentile converts still have to follow dietary restrictions, be wary of idolotry, and not allow sexual relations outside of marriage, but don't have to be circumcised

#### More on Paul

**Apologist:** Any of the Christian writers, primarily in the 2nd century, who attempted to provide a defense of Christianity and criticisms of paganism and other aspects of Greco-Roman culture. ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Apologist))

Paul wins in favour of Gentile Christians at Conference of Jerusalem, yay! → Cements him as ♥ *Apostle of the Gentiles* ♥

**Paul's success**
- Adapted himself to his audience
	- Could translate Palestinian Gospel into Greek (becomes first Christian Apologist)
	- Emphasized Christ as pre-existent rather than indicative of the apocalyptic end believed by the Palestinian Christians

**Paul's Church**
- Preached a one-ness of God
	- *Catholic*: comes from the Greek phrase καθόλου (katholou) 'on the whole, according to the whole, in general' [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_(term))
- Holy Society
	- Barrier between Jew and Gentile is broken down
- Jewish Christianity failed to convert Jewish people
	- Jerusalem badly damaged in 70 & 135 by Hadrian's edict
		- Jews excluded, becomes Greek city
