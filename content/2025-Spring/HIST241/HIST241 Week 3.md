## 2.19

**Jesus' Crucifiction**
- Regarded by Romans as potential violent revolutionary threat.
- Social radicalism
	- Meek inheriting the earth
- Romans didn't crucify willy-nilly
	- Reserved for worst-of-the-worst
- Rises three days later
	- Meets disciples
- The Great Commission (Matt 28:16-20)
	- calling on apostles to *spread the word*
	- baptise them!

**Ireneaus of Lyons** (100 CE)
- Doctrine of Apostolic Succession
	- Jesus passes authority to apostles to preach and baptise
	- Authority succeeds down the line (Peter to Pope)
	- Authority to "bind and to loose"
- Mathew 16:18: controversial passage
	- What did Jesus mean? Just Peter, or all apostles?

**Paul**
- Also died in Rome
- "Second founder of Christianity"
- Began life as very devout Jew
	- Pharisee, well educated
	- Spoke common Greek
- Persecutor of Christians
    - Jesus appears to him
    - Why you doing that??
- "Apostle to the Gentiles"

## 2.21

**Small, widespread Christian communities develop within a generation of Crucifiction**
- Paul
	- Second founder of Christianity
		- Reached out to broader audience, the Gentiles
		- Apostle to the Gentiles
	- Wrote letters!
		- First documents written that make it into New Testament
		- First New Testament writings
		- "First Christian Theologian"
		- Sought to make sense of, explain Jesus	
			- Life of Jesus wasn't so important
			- Living Christ was more important
				- *Less on the incarnation, more on the resurrected*
					- Gives us HOPE!
				- Mystery Cults promised salvation, centered around mythical figure
					- Jesus was real!!!
					- Relatable
			- Emphasizes ***faith** in the risen Christ*
				- Jesus lived again, so shall we
	- Believed in a second coming SOON
		- Hot apocalypticism
		- Gotta spread the word quick! He's coming!
 		- Things start to quiet down near end of 1st century
 		- 400, 1000, 1268: living in the last days!!!

	- Not formal biographies
	- Matthew, Mark, and Luke: *synoptic* Gospels
	- Christianity is an Abrahamic faith
		- Strictly monotheistic
		- Has a book!
	- Spreading, have to agree on something

		- Gotta have Greek!
	- Mark (symbol is the lion)

		- Probably closest to first Jewish revolt

	- Matthew
