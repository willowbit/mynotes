Who makes up the church?

Council of Arles
- Donatists declared to be heretical
- Schism continues
- Persecutions (Christians against Christians)

Arius the Heretic
- Priest in Alexandria
- God existed without the Son at one point
	- Beginning of time
	- Jesus is fully God, but not equal to God
- Bishop tells him to stop, doesn't
	- Schism
	- Arguing in the streets
- Constantine calls Council of Nicea

Council at Nicaea
- Attended by 318 bishops
- First universal conference
- Arius is wrong
	- Never was there a time where God the father existed without God the son
	- Of one substance
	- Gets exiled (but called back because Constantine is not quite sure)
		- Semi-Arian
- Defenders of Nicaea called Catholics
- Followers of Arian called Arians
- Constantius II
	- Arian, exiles Nicaea guy

Ufilas
- Goth
	- Barbarian peoples
	- Lived outside of empire
- Educated within empire
	- Learned Arian Christianity
- Returns to Goths, preaches Arian/Semi-Arian
	- Makes relationship with Catholic Rome more complicated
- Composed Gothic Bible
	- Invented Gothic alphabet/language
	- Left out war-like Kings

Julian
- Raised Christian, didn't believe it
- Publically displayed Pagan faith
- Rebuilt Jewish temple in Jerusalem
- Invades Persia... didn't work
