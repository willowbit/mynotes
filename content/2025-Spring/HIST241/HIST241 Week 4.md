**Gnosticism**
- Greek word for knowledge
- Claimed to have a special knowledge revealed to them by God
- Believed in a good god and a bad god (*dualism*)
- Believed the world was **bad**, evil
	- If the world is bad, and God is good, how to explain the world?
	- God is evil
		- We don't want to worship him, read him
		- Rejection of Hebrew Gospels
	- Jesus wasn't in the flesh, only appeared to be (*docetism*)
		- True God wouldn't appear in material world
		- Didn't actually die on the cross
		- Whole new perspective!
- Ultimately rejected
- Important because
	- Illustrate post-Christ discussion
	- Christianity develops over time, could have gone any direction
- **Marcion the Gnostic**
	- Goes to Rome to teach
	- Dualist, docetist
	- Codifies the Bible
		- Brings together collection of 11 books
		- Comes up with the idea of the standard text
			- Letters of Paul, version of Mark, rejected Hebrew scriptures
	- Central ideas rejected, but good idea of standardizing!
- **Muratorian Canon**
	- Earliest known list of the New Testament
		- 4 Gospels
		- Acts of the Apostles
		- Most of Epistles
		- John and Peter's Apocalypses
		- Book of Wisdom
	- Implies authority: someone has to say what's included

Understanding that Old Testament God is same God Jesus talks about.
- Prophesizes coming of Christ
- Gotta include it!

**Council of Jerusalem** (Acts 15)

**Irenaeus of Lyons**

## 2.26

We need institutions and structures to define what is right Christian belief.

The Phrygian Heresy (*The New Prophecy, the Montanist prophecy*)
- Believed that God continues to reveal himself
- New messages are coming!
- Speaking in tongues, trances
- Including women
- Don't need a Bishop if God is speaking directly to you

Montanists, Gnostics: show variety of Christianity as it develops.

Mainstream Christianity is shaped through its debates with other Christian groups
- Irenaeus emphasizes Jesus as God in the flesh against Gnostics.

The Bishop
- Receives money from Christians
- Establishment of wellfare programs
- Visible through construction of catacombs
	- Not only poor people participating in movement
	- Early expressions of Christian art
		- Not Jesus-specific
		- Christians seen as antisocial, separate
		- Fish, dove as an ambiguous symbol

The Apologists
- Wrote apologeia
- Justin trained as a pagan philosopher
	- Plato, Aristotle, etc.
	- Wasn't satisfied with answer
	- Converts, brings Greek philosophy to it
- Effort to explain Christianity in a way others can understand

Tertullian
- First of the Latin fathers
- Attacked Marcion and heretics
- Wrote *Adversus Iudaeos* criticising Judaism
- What has Athens to do with Jerusalem?
	- How is classical learning, lit, philosophy useful?
	- IT'S NOT!!! It's Pagan

Origen (c. 184-c. 253)
- Trained in Alexandria
- Well versed in Greek thought, Neo-Platonist
- Greatest of Christian theologians
- God created all things, all things are good, all things will return to God
	- Even the devil will return to God, controversial
- Applied ideas of allegory to entire bible·
